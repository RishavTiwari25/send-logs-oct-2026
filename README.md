# Send log: Oct to 1 Nov 2026
Outbound mails from the BIT/personal mailboxes, 22 Oct to 1 Nov 2026 (IST).
Sources: hunter-state/sends.json (22-31 Oct), leads/q01nov_sends.txt (1 Nov; UTC stamps converted to IST).
No log exists for 1-21 Oct. 1 Nov rows have no subject recorded.


## November 2026
`november-2026-send-log.csv`: every Nov send with per-thread follow-up dates (FU1/FU2), reply, status. Excludes 6sense and Nirmata. Gmail thread IDs were not recorded locally; thread key = recipient email.


## November 2026 replies
`november-2026-replies-log.csv`: replies received to November cold mails in the btech10174 mailbox (inbox threads, 1-24 Nov), summarised. Excludes bounces. Date = last activity in the thread. Archived replies and the personal mailbox are not covered.
